\section{Related Work}
\textbf{Dynamic and adaptive inference.} Early-exit networks~\cite{teerapittayanon2016branchynet}
and the broader family of dynamic networks~\cite{han2022dynamic} trade accuracy
for compute per input, and once-for-all training~\cite{cai2020ofa} derives many
deployable sub-networks from one model. Quantisation~\cite{jacob2018quant}
provides cheaper operating points; its speed benefit depends on the runtime and
CPU, so we measure it rather than assume it. \shade{} is orthogonal: it consumes
whatever operating points exist and decides \emph{when} to move between them.

\textbf{Video detection and tracking.} Exploiting temporal redundancy by
propagating features~\cite{zhu2017dff} or boxes~\cite{bewley2016sort} between key
frames is well established. Our shifter is deliberately simpler than a tracker:
per-box translation by phase correlation~\cite{kuglin1975phase} on a small
grey image, anchored at each real detection. Its novelty is its coupling to a
thermal budget (Sec.~\ref{sec:sched}), not the propagation primitive.
