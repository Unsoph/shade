\section{Conclusion}
\shade{} packages a degradation ladder, guarded recovery, hot-swap,
supervision and an audit log for edge detectors, adds a scheduler that spends the
detection budget on scene change, and ships a simulator-based method for
ablating adaptation policies. Simulation suggests that adaptation buys
predictability rather than throughput, and that simple guarded reactive control is
