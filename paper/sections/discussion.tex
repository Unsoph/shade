\section{Discussion}
\textbf{When does adaptation pay?} Our simulation says the answer depends on
the cost of throttling relative to the quality given up on lower rungs, and on
whether the application values frame-rate predictability. The hardware
experiments must establish the throttle penalty on the target
(\TBD{measured frame-rate ratio throttled/unthrottled}).

\textbf{Auditability.} Every rung change carries reasons and signal values,
which supports post-incident review; we have not evaluated this with operators.

\textbf{Limitations.} Single device family \TBD{and one or two boards}; one
detector family; global-motion-biased scheduling evidence unless E8 covers
independent motion; thresholds untuned; the 320 and INT8 rungs depend on the
user's calibration data; the HTTP status server is unauthenticated and
local-only by default; no Jetson backend exists yet.

\textbf{Safety.} \shade{} is not a safety-certified system. Degrading a
detector can degrade downstream decisions; applications must set the minimum
acceptable rung (\TBD{discuss floor configuration}).
